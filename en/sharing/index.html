Sharing

Sharing your enthusiasm for Raku with others

So you enjoy Raku, and want to let others know about it? There are a variety of ways you can do this.

The marketing site has some resources sorely in need of an update (talk to the Outreach Troupe to assist). At this point, the only useful material is the logos, which you may be able to use in your efforts to share your love of Raku.

Books/Merch/Financial Support§

  • Books don't directly support Raku itself, but they support other members of the Raku community, Note that a number also refer to it as Perl 6, which is the old name.
  • A few select books can be found at https://raku.org/learn
  • A larger selection can be found at https://perl6book.com/
  • Neither of the above lists includes Raku Recipes
  • Merch: You can also get some merch at Zazzle Raku/Rakudo Merch.
  • Donations can be made to the Raku Foundation, which supports events, grants, and helps keep the Raku infrastructure running,

Learning/Writing about Raku§

Sometimes the best way to learn about something is to write about it, You can read more about this approach on the Writing to Learn page.
